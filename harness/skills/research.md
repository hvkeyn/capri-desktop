# Research

The job is to end up with facts someone can check, not with the feeling of having read the news.

- **Search before you guess.** `web_search` first, then `web_fetch` on the addresses worth reading. Two
  or three good sources beat ten.
- **Go to the one who said it.** An agency, a ministry, a company's own release, a court's own file. A
  retelling of a retelling is not a source: name it as a retelling.
- **A feed is a list of titles, not the story.** Open the item you are going to use.
- **A site that refuses is a site to leave.** 401, 403, a timeout, a paywall: say so in one line and move
  on to the next. Two sources out of three answering is a finished step, not a failed one.
- **Numbers carry their unit and their moment.** A price at a time, a count on a date, a percentage of
  what. "A record" with no number is not a fact.
- **Write down the four things** with every item: the claim, who says it, when it was published, the
  address. That is what makes it checkable later.
- **Confirm the claim you will print**, not the topic. Two sources on the same event at different
  moments of it are still two sources.
- **Say what you could not check.** A line about the gap is worth more than a confident sentence you
  cannot back.
